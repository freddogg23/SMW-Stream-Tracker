Release 2.1.0

* Adds a dedicated SNES ROMs tab with cleaned names, folder-wide importing, matching search and filters, bulk selection and deletion, and MiSTer SD-card support.
* Extends RetroAchievements identification to imported SNES games with trophy markers and complete official badge, requirement, point, and unlock details.
* Adds dated new-content tracking and notification dots for moderated and waiting SMW Central hacks.
* Adds named MiSTer profiles, automatic online-console failover, and automatic Ethernet/Wi-Fi address discovery.
* Speeds up verified bulk MiSTer transfers and makes Find & Set Up MiSTer install virtual save-state slots 5-11 automatically.
* Adds approved Streamer.bot tracker controls, suggested one-click mappings, and loop prevention alongside the existing outbound events.
* Adds adaptive polling, crash recovery, automatic backups, library maintenance, post-stream summaries, completion detection, and automatic statistic reconciliation.
* Adds Always Ask to Confirm Final Exit and only suggests completion when the final exit and completion state agree.
* Hides SMW-only creator and exit information for ordinary SNES games while retaining names, deaths, and timers.
* Rejects suspicious deaths during pipes, doors, transitions, loading, and save-state restoration, including RA-SNES/SNI sessions.
* Fits MiSTer and RetroArch Platform settings on single pages and modernizes the remaining setup and detail dialogs.
* Publishes Windows application, installer, updater, source, and checksums only. macOS remains untested and is not included.
