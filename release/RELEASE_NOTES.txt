Release 1.0.6

* Shows Help and About update indicators only when a newer published release is available.
* Adds filtered random play for downloaded and patched ROMs with Confirm and Cancel controls.
* Detects playable catalog games from direct paths, platform mappings, FXPAK mappings, and the configured ROM library.
* Makes playable catalog titles yellow and launchable from the catalog.
* Adds navigation between Overview and My Tracker and moves spreadsheet tools into My Tracker.
* Improves full-screen window scaling, filter alignment, and translations.
* Fixes the Tk update-badge startup crash.
