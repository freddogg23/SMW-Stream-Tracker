Release 1.1.0

* Keeps the level timer through older-hack death-to-overworld transitions and resets it only after a confirmed finish or a different level selection.
* Adds search and click-to-sort behavior throughout catalog and tracker lists, plus combined download filters for difficulty, type, and rating.
* Adds clipboard editing to My Tracker and the renamed Add Unmoderated Hack window.
* Preserves existing appearance choices while giving first-time users the new tracker colors and data-bar defaults.
* Adds an in-app Help menu with the installed-language guide, SMW Central links and credits, About and Updates, and anonymous feedback.
* Keeps the embedded feedback form synchronized with light and dark appearance changes.
* Moves Appearance into Settings and improves update, download, and installer dialog sizing and file selection.
* Expands the setup guides with LiveSplit, OBS Studio, and Streamlabs Desktop instructions.
* Removes legacy signed v1.0.2 build artifacts and personal publisher references from the release source.
