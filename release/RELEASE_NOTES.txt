Release 1.0.0

* First public release of SMW Stream Tracker.
* Supports always-on FXPAK Pro and RetroArch tracking.
* Includes the searchable SMW Central catalog, filtered missing-hack downloads, tracker statistics, exports, backups, and stream timer output.
* Colors only Difficulty cells in Progress by Difficulty.
* Displays Rate as an editable, value-based gradient data bar.
* Adds persistent solid, gradient, alternating-row, Overview, and default appearance controls to every application table.
* Includes searchable and sortable lists, centered table contents, clickable SMW Central page and patch links, and horizontal scrolling where needed.
* Keeps the level timer through older-hack death-to-overworld transitions and resets it only after a confirmed finish or a different level selection.
* Preserves user appearance choices and supplies first-use tracker colors and data-bar defaults.
* Includes multilingual setup guides for LiveSplit, OBS Studio, and Streamlabs Desktop.
* Includes in-app Help, About and Updates, SMW Central credits, and anonymous Feedback & Suggestions.
* Distributes unsigned installers with SHA-256 verification and rollback-capable in-app updates.
