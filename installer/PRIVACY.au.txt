SMW STREAM TRACKER PRIVACY — THE FAIR DINKUM VERSION
=====================================================

SMW Stream Tracker does not collect telemetry and does not upload ROM files.
No funny business, mate.

The app uses the network only for features you request or enable:

* SMW Central catalogue and moderated patch information.
* GitHub release checks and checksum-verified updater downloads.
* Optional Google Sheets synchronisation through the URL you supply.
* Optional installer downloads for SNI, QUsb2Snes, RetroArch, and Libretro cores.
* Local communication with SNI, QUsb2Snes, FXPAK Pro, or RetroArch.
* The optional Microsoft Forms feedback page you choose to open.

Feedback & Suggestions opens a public Microsoft Forms page inside a dedicated
SMW Stream Tracker web-view window. If the embedded web view is unavailable,
the same form opens in your default browser. The form does not ask for your name or email
address. The app does not attach settings, diagnostics, ROM information, file
paths, or tracker data. Only the answers you enter into the form are submitted.

Microsoft may process normal service data under its privacy policy when the form
is opened or submitted. SMW Stream Tracker does not receive or store that service
data.

Tracker data, settings, timer state, mappings, recovery backups, and error logs
are stored locally. Error logs may contain technical file paths, but Copy
Diagnostics redacts usernames, ROM names, and personal paths before placing a
report on the clipboard.

SMW Stream Tracker never includes, downloads, or uploads a commercial base ROM.
Too right — your ROM stays yours.
