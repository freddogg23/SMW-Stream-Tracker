Release 2.2.1

* Refreshes application-audio sources from the Apps section of Windows Volume Mixer across every active playback output, including applications routed to secondary devices.
* Performs a fresh source scan on every click, prioritizes active sessions, groups duplicate processes, and keeps the interface responsive while scanning.
* Uses the online shared SMW Central fingerprint catalog without bundling or maintaining a full offline music index; new and changed submissions refresh in the cloud.
* Improves repeat song identification, voice-aware listening, community song result wording, and rapid consecutive death counting.
* Publishes Windows application, installer, updater, source, and checksums only. macOS remains untested and is not included.
