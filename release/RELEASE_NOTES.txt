Release 1.0.4

* Prevents Mario A/B/C save-slot life synchronization from adding a false total death.
* Counts the first real death after quitting, reopening, or switching save files.
* Restores death tracking immediately when returning directly to a previously started level.
* Improves translated privacy warnings, connection messages, and file-tool labels.
* Expands regression coverage for death tracking, downloaded-only random play, service health, and streamer privacy.
* Preserves the existing privacy-safe screenshots without replacing them in this maintenance release.
